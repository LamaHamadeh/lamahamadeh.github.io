Physics Is Nature – Instagram Page

Aims of the page:

• Deliver and simplify physics ideas, methods, theories and facts.

• Show the intimate relation between physics as a science and natural phenomena.

• Highlight the importance of physics to society today, particularly through our reliance on technology and the many technologies that are continually transforming our world and can be directly traced back to important physics research.

IOP 3 Minute Wonder

IOP 3 Minute Wonder is the national science communication competition hosted by the Institute of Physics (IOP).

Achievement: Winner of the East Midlands branch and UK finalist in the final round of the competition that took place at The Royal Institution in London.

Talk title: The exciting world of smart textiles.

Meet a Physicist

Online event hosted by the Institute of Physics (IOP) East Midlands.

Role: Member of a panel that consisted of three other female academics.

Indicator of Impact: Engagement with a national working group, i.e. IOP and Girlguiding Nottinghamshire.

Audience: Girls and young women, primarily in school, of age range 7 years old to 15 years old.

Spring into STEM

Event: Online lecture hosted by UCL Engineering. This lecture was part of a series of virtual lectures that aim to showcase the world-changing STEM research from across UCL Engineering departments.

Role: Providing a presentation talking about my research and answering questions from students who would like to apply to UCL.

Audience: High school students interested in joining the Mechanical Engineering Department.

Talk title: Machine Learning as a Potential Tool for Disease Diagnosis.

STEM Ambassador

STEM outreach visits to schools of under-represented groups to widen diversity and promote subjects in STEM subjects.

Delivered a talk to Year 12 and Year 13 students from under-represented groups, i.e. girls, at Nottingham Girls Academy.

Talk title: STEM in Life and its Wide-Ranging Career Options & Celebrating Women in STEM

Event: BIG Bang STEM All-Day Event.

Role: Present and explain student-built designs from the UCL Mechanical Engineering Department to high school students from under-represented groups, particularly girls.
