Teaching

Teaching, supervision and engineering education research

My teaching experience spans over eight years delivering courses in physics, mathematics, and scientific computing across various departments and student backgrounds.

My teaching approach focuses on adopting a research-led teaching model that engages students with contemporary research challenges and fosters an inquiry-driven learning environment.

My teaching responsibilities at UCL Mechanical Engineering span across multiple year groups and include the following modules:

Engineering Education Research

My engineering education research explores evidence-based strategies to improve student learning outcomes, with a particular focus on integrating programming, data-driven techniques, and generative artificial intelligence tools into students' learning processes.

My research has been disseminated through international conferences, including the following:

Held at: Rosenheim Technical University of Applied Sciences, Rosenheim, Germany.

Talk: Investigate Students' Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools in Engineering Programming Assessment.

Conference Proceedings: PTEE 2024 Abstracts

9th International Research Symposium on Problem-Based Learning: Transforming Engineering Education 2023 (TEE2023)

Talk: Integrating Python into Mechanical Engineering Undergraduate Curriculum.

Conference Proceedings: Transforming Engineering Education
