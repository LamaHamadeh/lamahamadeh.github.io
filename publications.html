Machine Learning Analysis for Quantitative Discrimination of Dried Blood Droplets

Using an optimised machine learning algorithm to analyse and classify dried patterns from blood droplets.

Hamadeh, L.; Imran, S.; Bencsik, M.; Sharpe, G. R.; Johnson, M. A.; Fairhurst, D. J. (2020). Machine Learning Analysis for Quantitative Discrimination of Dried Blood Droplets. Scientific Reports, 10, Article 3313. 10.1038/s41598-020-59847-x

Significance: The paper was featured in News Medical Life Sciences .

Towards reliable smart textiles: Investigating thermal characterisation of embedded electronics in E-Textiles using infrared thermography and mathematical modelling

Infrared thermographic imaging technology is utilised to experimentally study the thermal distribution profile of LEDs in their bare, encapsulated and embedded state in e-yarns manufacturing stages. The experimental results are compared with numerical analysis models solving the time-dependent partial differential equations of the heat transfer process.

Hamadeh, Lama; Al-Habaibeh, Amin (2022). Towards reliable smart textiles: Investigating thermal characterisation of embedded electronics in E-Textiles using infrared thermography and mathematical modelling. Sensors and Actuators A: Physical , 338, Article 113501. 10.1016/j.sna.2022.113501

Enhancing the sustainability and energy conservation in heritage buildings: The case of Nottingham Playhouse

A novel methodology was developed to improve the energy performance of heritage buildings, demonstrated using infrared thermographic images and data logger sensors to identify significant energy savings.

Al-Habaibeh, A.; Hawas, A.; Hamadeh, L.; Medjdoub, B.; Marsh, J.; Sen, A. (2021). Enhancing the sustainability and energy conservation in heritage buildings: The case of Nottingham Playhouse. Frontiers of Architectural Research . 10.1016/j.foar.2021.09.001

Significance: The paper was awarded the Frontiers of Architectural Research Best Paper in 2022 .

Exploring the Utilisation of Generative AI Tools by Undergraduate First-Year Mechanical Engineering Students in Programming Assessments

This paper investigates first-year undergraduate mechanical engineering students' use of Generative AI tools in their programming assessment.

Hamadeh, Lama (2024). Exploring the Utilisation of Generative AI Tools by Undergraduate First-Year Mechanical Engineering Students in Programming Assessments. SEFI Journal of Engineering Education Advancement , 1(1), pp. 39–52. 10.62492/sefijeea.v1i1.20
