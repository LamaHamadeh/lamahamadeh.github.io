About Me

I am a Lecturer in Engineering Mathematics and Computing in the Mechanical Engineering Department at UCL. I serve as the Director of the MSc Engineering with Finance Programme and Chair of the Undergraduate Board of Examiners. I lead multiple undergraduate and postgraduate modules within the department and am a Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA)

I am a certified Mental Health First Aider, trained through an MHFA England-accredited course. In 2025, I received the Faculty Education Award and was shortlisted for the UCL Provost’s Education Award in the Innovation category.

I joined the Mechanical Engineering Department at UCL in 2021. Before joining UCL, I held the following academic and research positions at Nottingham Trent University:

  1. Lecturer in Physics and Mathematics
    Department of Physics and Mathematics, School of Science & Technology, Nottingham Trent University.
  2. Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Physics
    Department of Physics and Mathematics, School of Science & Technology, Nottingham Trent University.
  3. Research Assistant
    Department of Product Design, School of Architecture, Design & the Built Environment, Nottingham Trent University.
  4. Visiting Research Scholar
    Department of Product Design, School of Architecture, Design & the Built Environment, Nottingham Trent University.
I earned my PhD in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics from the University of Nottingham in 2015.
