About Me

I am a Lecturer in Engineering Mathematics and Computing at UCL. I serve as the Director of the MSc Engineering with Finance Programme and Chair of the Undergraduate Board of Examiners. I lead multiple undergraduate and postgraduate modules within the department and am a Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA) .

In addition, I am a certified Mental Health First Aider, trained through an MHFA England-accredited course. In 2025, I received the Faculty Education Award and was shortlisted for the UCL Provost’s Education Award in the Innovation category.

I joined the Mechanical Engineering Department at UCL in 2021. Before that, I was appointed Lecturer in Physics and Mathematics at Nottingham Trent University in 2018, following a postdoctoral research fellowship in the same department.

From 2017 to 2018, I focused on developing advanced image analysis methods to statistically and objectively analyse patterns in a large dataset of dried blood droplet images.

Between 2015 and 2017, I worked as a Research Assistant in the Product Design Department at Nottingham Trent University’s School of Architecture, Design & the Built Environment. I contributed to an EPSRC-funded project on the mathematical modelling of the thermal behaviour of electronic components in smart textiles.

I earned my PhD in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics from the University of Nottingham in 2015.
